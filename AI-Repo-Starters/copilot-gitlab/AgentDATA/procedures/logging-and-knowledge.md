# Recording evidence and reusable findings

Paths below refer to AgentDATA unless stated otherwise.

Use logs/ for dated execution evidence, memories/ for dated session observations,
Knowledge/ for maintained technical findings and context/handoff.md for the next
action. Do not copy the same narrative into every folder. Record the source,
scope and limitations; distinguish measured facts from inference. Redact secrets
and personal data. Supersede stale knowledge explicitly rather than presenting
an old observation as a permanent rule. Templates contain no actual execution.
