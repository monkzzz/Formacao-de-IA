# Decision register

Owner: the participant approves material product choices; the agent records them.
Routine reversible implementation choices may be resolved within the authorized
task and recorded with their rationale. Silence is not approval.

For each actual decision record:

- ID, date, status (`proposed`, `accepted`, `superseded`) and responsible person.
- Question, relevant facts, alternatives, selected choice and rationale.
- Approval or authority source, consequences, linked evidence and review trigger.
- Replacement decision ID when superseded; preserve the original history.
